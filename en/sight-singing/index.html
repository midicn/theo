Sight-Singing

Singing from the page — it forces you to pre-hear before you make a sound

1 min read54 characters条目Ear TrainingUpdated 2026-09-25

Sight-singing means singing straight from the page. Its value is not performance but training one thing:

It forces you to pre-hear before you make a sound.

This is exactly layer 4 of ear training: seeing the page and knowing the sound. Sight-singing is how that layer is trained. So the question is not whether you sing beautifully, but whether you hear it inwardly first.

The two solfège systems

This is the most important choice in sight-singing, and the two emphasise different things:

Movable doFixed do
Where do sitsmoves with the tonicalways C
What it strengthensrelations within the key (degrees and function)absolute pitch and reading speed
What it resemblesthe logic of numbered notation (see jianpu)the "position" logic of the staff
Common infolk, vocal, pop, Chinese music teachinginstrumental, classical, conservatory training
Weaknessweaker absolute pitch sense after transpositionweaker intuitive sense of degree relations

A recommendation: learn both, with one as primary —

AimPriority
understanding degree relations and harmonic functionmovable do
instrumental reading, complex accidentalsfixed do

Neither system is more correct. They are two mappings: one takes the degree as coordinate, the other absolute pitch.

A four-step method

StepDo thisWhy
1 read clef, key and metreestablish the pitch range and time framesinging without this is singing blind
2 find the tonic and scalebuild the map of the key inwardlyevery degree relation is measured from the tonic
3 mark the difficultiesleaps, accidentals, syncopationsthese are the exact places errors occur
4 conduct while singingbeat time with the hand as you singholds the temporal and pitch dimensions at once

Sight-singing errors cluster almost entirely on leaps and accidentals. Sing those alone twice, then the whole phrase; the success rate rises sharply.

Without conducting, the rhythm deforms with difficulty: hard notes drag, easy ones hurry. Conducting nails the rhythm down.

Why it is useful to every musician

UseExplanation
the core method of ear trainingsee above: it trains pre-hearing
reading speedunderstanding is not the same as singing; only what you can sing is truly read
inner hearinghearing the sound inwardly while reading (the basis of composing and arranging)
choirs and ensemblessing a new part to yourself and you know where it is hard (see sight-reading)

Inner hearing is a basic skill of composing and arranging. Without it, writing becomes trial and error: write the notes, play them back, listen.

Listen: pre-hear before you make a sound

The core act of sight-singing is hearing inwardly first, then singing. Use interval for this exercise: note what a and b are, pre-hear the interval inwardly, then play it to check.

Common misconceptions

  • "Sight-singing means singing accurately." The point is hearing it inwardly first. Accuracy is the result; pre-hearing is the ability.
  • "You must choose one solfège system." They train different abilities; ideally learn both, with one primary.
  • "Sight-singing and sight-reading are the same." One is singing (training inner hearing), the other playing (training reading and response) — see sight-reading.
  • "Sight-singing takes a long time to pay off." It shows quickly: marking the leaps and accidentals first alone reduces errors immediately.
